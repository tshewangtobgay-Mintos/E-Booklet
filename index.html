About This E-Booklet

Purpose

This e-booklet presents a comprehensive overview of Bhutan's ICT industry, showcasing their 25+ locally developed or customized digital products and services. The booklet is designed to serve as an official industry catalogue and reference guide for the technology sector in Bhutan.

For Whom

This publication is intended for government agencies, private companies, small and medium enterprises, startups, educational institutions, NGOs, development partners, investors, and individuals seeking to identify and adopt relevant IT solutions available within Bhutan.

What You Will Find

Each company profile includes detailed information about locally developed products and services, target user groups, current clients, technological capabilities, sector applications, and direct contact information. The booklet concludes with comprehensive indexes organized by company, technology area, and sector.

Local Development

A significant percentage of the solutions featured in this booklet have been locally developed by Bhutanese teams, reflecting the growing capability and innovation within Bhutan's ICT industry. Some solutions are internationally sourced but customized for the Bhutanese market context.

Disclaimer: Company information, products, users, sectors and services presented in this booklet are based on information submitted through the 2026 ICT products and services data collection. Technology classifications and use cases should be understood as descriptive categorizations and not as independent technical certification of the products. For detailed technical specifications, implementation support, or trial access, interested organizations should contact the companies directly using the contact information provided.
