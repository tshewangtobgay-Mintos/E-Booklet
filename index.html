About This E-Booklet

Purpose

A comprehensive overview of Bhutan's ICT industry, showcasing 30+ locally developed or customized digital products and services — an official industry catalogue and reference guide for the technology sector.

For Whom

Government agencies, private companies, SMEs, startups, educational institutions, NGOs, development partners, investors and individuals seeking relevant IT solutions available within Bhutan.

What You Will Find

Company profiles with products, target users, clients, technology capabilities, sector applications and direct contacts — plus indexes organised by company, technology area and sector.

Local Development

A significant share of the solutions are built by Bhutanese teams, reflecting growing innovation in the sector. Some are internationally sourced but customised for the Bhutanese market.

Disclaimer: Company information, products, users, sectors and services are based on the 2026 ICT products and services data collection. Technology classifications are descriptive and not independent technical certification. For specifications, implementation support or trial access, contact the companies directly.
